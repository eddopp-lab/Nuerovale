About Us

Neurovale is a Behaviour Support organisation dedicated to helping individuals build emotional regulation, strengthen relationships, and achieve meaningful growth. We specialise in Behaviour Support and Counselling under the NDIS, working with participants of all ages and backgrounds.

Our team of qualified practitioners brings warmth, expertise, and genuine care to every interaction — because we know that meaningful change begins with feeling truly heard and understood.

What We Offer

At Neurovale, we believe meaningful change comes through compassion and understanding. Our services are designed to empower individuals through evidence-based, person-centred Behaviour Support and Counselling under the NDIS.

We work collaboratively with participants, families, and support networks to create lasting positive outcomes.

Behaviour Support

Positive behaviour support planning tailored to each individual, focusing on building skills and reducing behaviours of concern.

Counselling

Compassionate, evidence-based counselling to support emotional wellbeing, build resilience, and navigate life's challenges.

NDIS Support

NDIS-registered services that work within your plan to deliver meaningful, goal-oriented support and greater independence.
