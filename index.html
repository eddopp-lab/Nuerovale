About Neurovale

Empowering Positive
Change

Neurovale is a Behaviour Support organisation dedicated to helping individuals build emotional regulation, strengthen relationships, and achieve meaningful growth. We specialise in Behaviour Support and Counselling under the NDIS — guided by empathy, respect, and a genuine belief that every person deserves to thrive.

Person-Centred

Every plan and intervention is tailored to the unique needs, goals, and strengths of each individual.

Evidence-Based

Our practice is grounded in the latest research in positive behaviour support and therapeutic counselling.

Collaborative

We work alongside participants, families, and support networks to create lasting positive outcomes.

Our Services

What We Offer

01

Behaviour Support

Positive behaviour support planning tailored to each individual. We work to understand the function of behaviour, build meaningful skills, and reduce behaviours of concern through compassionate, evidence-based strategies.

02

Counselling

Compassionate, evidence-based counselling to support emotional wellbeing and build resilience. We help individuals and families navigate life's challenges with confidence, clarity, and self-understanding.

03

NDIS Support

As an NDIS registered provider, we work within your plan to deliver meaningful, goal-oriented support. We help participants understand their funding, access the right services, and achieve greater independence.
